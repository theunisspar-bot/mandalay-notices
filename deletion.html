This page explains how to have your information deleted from the internal tools described in our privacy policy.

How to ask

Write to theunisspar@gmail.com from any address, or tell your store manager and ask them to send the request. Give us your name and the work mobile number we message you on, so we can find the right records. No account or login is needed.

What happens next

  • We confirm the request within 5 working days.
  • Your contact number is removed from the messaging lists straight away, so the WhatsApp messages stop.
  • Your name, contact number and any photographs of you are deleted within 30 days.
  • We write back to confirm what was deleted.

What we may keep

Operational records that we are required to keep — payroll and overtime records, disciplinary records, and anything needed for a legal or audit requirement — are retained for as long as the law requires, even after a deletion request. Where we keep such a record we tell you which one and why.

Stopping messages only

If you only want the WhatsApp messages to stop and would rather we keep your employment records as they are, say so in your request and we will remove your number without deleting anything else.

If you are not satisfied

Under POPIA you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa. You can also ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, at the same address above.
