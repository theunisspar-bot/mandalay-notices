Privacy policy

Mandalay Group Ten (Pty) Ltd, registration 2020/681991/07, Shop 88 Zevenwacht Mall, Cnr Van Riebeeck and Polka Draai Roads, Kuils River, 7580, South Africa.

This policy covers the internal tools the company uses to run its stores, including the department screens, maintenance reporting and the WhatsApp messages sent to staff and contractors.

What we process

  • Names and work contact numbers of staff, managers and contractors.
  • Operational records: maintenance jobs, production and stock entries, cleaning sign-offs, customer orders taken in store, and photographs attached to those records.
  • Delivery information for messages we send (sent, delivered, read).

Why

To run the stores: to tell the right person that equipment is broken, to plan and record production, and to keep a record of who did what and when. We rely on our legitimate interest in operating the business and on our obligations as an employer.

Sharing

We use Google (Workspace and Apps Script) to host the tools and Meta Platforms to deliver WhatsApp messages. We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Keeping it

Operational records are kept while they are needed for the business and for any legal or audit requirement. Photographs taken to confirm who performed an action are deleted after 60 days.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or object to processing. Write to theunisspar@gmail.com and we will respond.

Messages

Staff and contractors receive WhatsApp messages about their work, such as maintenance jobs and reminders. To stop receiving them, tell your manager and we will remove your number.
